01
Fire Resistant
Tekton poles do not combust. Engineered to total loss of structural capacity at temperatures concrete and steel fail. Eliminates the fire risk at the infrastructure level.
02
Production Speed
One 40-foot pole every 5 minutes at full capacity — 84 poles per 8-hour shift. No competitor in earth climates comes close to that throughput.
03
Zero Environmental Footprint
Zero water used in production. Zero trees harvested. Zero chemical preservatives — no contamination risk from installation day to decommission.
04
Impervious to Elements
Immune to rot, corrosion, insects, UV, humidity, and saltwater. Unlike wood and steel, Tekton poles reach extreme environments without maintenance or structural compromise.
05
Lightweight Install
Significantly lighter than steel and concrete alternatives. Tekton poles reduce installation labor and equipment costs, dramatically reducing total deployment cost.
06
Standards Compliant
Designed to meet NESC, RUS, and ANSI O5.1 utility industry standards — a drop-in replacement with an upgrade in every measurable dimension.
