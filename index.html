The Product

Tekton Pole & Pipe

Built on the proprietary Theis Tekton material system — advanced composite poles and pipes engineered for the demands of 21st-century utility infrastructure.

🔥

Fire-Resistant

Class 4 utility poles up to 65 feet that do not combust. Engineered to stop the next wildfire before it starts.

⚡

Production Speed

One 40-foot pole every 5 minutes at full production — 84 poles per 8-hour shift, per line. No competitor comes close.

🌿

Truly Sustainable

Zero water consumption in production. Remarkably low CO₂ emissions. Green cradle to grave. LEED and EPA friendly.

Impervious

Immune to rot, termites, UV rays, and extreme weather. Built to outlast wood poles by decades with minimal maintenance.

Lightweight Install

No heavy equipment needed. Lightweight design cuts install time and cost, making crews faster and projects cheaper.

Regulatory Compliant

Fully compliant with NESC, RUS, and ANSI utility pole standards. Ready for immediate deployment on approval.
