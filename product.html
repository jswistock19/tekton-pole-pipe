Technical Specifications

Every spec. Every
advantage.

Tekton poles are engineered to exceed utility industry standards across every dimension — from structural performance to environmental impact to installation logistics.

🔥

Fire Resistance

Extremely high fire resistance — class-leading performance in the utility pole market. Where wood poles become accelerants and composite poles melt, Tekton holds the line.

  • Does not combust or support flame spread
  • Maintains structural integrity under extreme heat exposure
  • Validated for deployment in Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) zones
  • Dramatically reduces ignition risk from downed power lines
⚡

Production Capacity

The most efficient utility pole production system ever built. TPP's manufacturing process is orders of magnitude faster than wood, steel, or standard composite alternatives.

  • One 40-foot pole every 5 minutes at full production
  • 84 poles per 8-hour shift, per manufacturing line
  • Scalable: additional lines multiply output linearly
  • Wood poles take days to weeks — steel and concrete take even longer
🌿

Environmental Performance

Zero water consumption in production. Remarkably low CO₂ output. No chemical treatments that leach into soil or groundwater. No deforestation. No hazardous disposal.

  • 0 gallons of water used in manufacturing
  • 330 kg CO₂ per 1,000 poles (steel: 1,460 | wood: 867)
  • No toxic preservatives (no CCA, creosote, or penta)
  • Zero deforestation — no harvested trees required
  • Recyclable end-of-life pathway
🛡️

Durability & Longevity

Engineered for the rigors of real-world utility infrastructure. Tekton poles outlast their wood counterparts by more than double — with virtually no maintenance required.

  • 75+ year estimated service life (wood: 30–40 years)
  • Immune to rot, fungal decay, and insect infestation
  • Resistant to UV radiation, extreme temperatures, and saltwater
  • High mass-to-energy absorption for impact and wind events
  • No periodic retreatment or inspection intervals for preservatives
🏗️

Installation & Handling

Lightweight construction means faster, cheaper installation. No specialized heavy equipment required. Every crew benefits from reduced labor time and reduced jobsite risk.

  • Significantly lighter than steel and concrete alternatives
  • No heavy crane or specialized lift equipment required
  • Zero handling damage risk — superior to composite competitors
  • Reduces installation labor hours and associated cost
  • Compatible with standard pole-setting equipment
📐

Standards & Compliance

Designed from the ground up to meet the regulatory requirements of the US utility industry. Tekton poles are engineered to comply with every major standard governing utility pole deployment.

  • Designed for NESC (National Electrical Safety Code) compliance
  • RUS (USDA Rural Utilities Service) standards-aligned
  • ANSI O5.1 utility pole standards-aligned
  • Third-party testing and validation pathway in progress
  • Internal grounding capability — eliminates copper theft incentive
