02.1Material Science

WHAT WE BUILD WITH

Tekton poles are engineered from a proprietary carbon fiber composite matrix — the same class of material used in aerospace structures and advanced defense platforms. The result is a pole that carries loads wood can't dream of, in conditions steel corrodes in, for decades nothing else survives.

—Carbon Fiber Composite

THE CORE MATERIAL

Our proprietary composite matrix bonds high-tensile carbon fiber with advanced polymer resins — engineered for dielectric strength, UV resistance, extreme weather endurance, and zero water absorption. This isn't modified wood or galvanized steel. It's a generational leap in materials science applied to the most fundamental infrastructure on earth.

  • Tensile strength exceeds structural steel at a fraction of the weight
  • Fully dielectric — no electrical conductivity, no grounding risk
  • Zero water absorption — no rot, no swelling, no degradation
  • UV stabilized for harsh solar environments
  • Non-magnetic — compatible with sensitive grid sensing equipment
—Manufacturing Precision

BUILT IN AMERICA

Every Tekton pole is manufactured in our San Antonio, Texas facility using a controlled filament winding and pultrusion process. Each unit undergoes dimensional verification, load testing, and surface finishing before it leaves the floor. Our current capacity: 84 poles per shift — scaling to meet grid-scale demand.

  • Controlled filament winding process for consistent wall geometry
  • Load-tested to rated capacity before shipment
  • Produced in San Antonio, TX + Little Rock, AR
  • 84 poles per shift — scalable production
  • Made in the USA — domestic supply chain
