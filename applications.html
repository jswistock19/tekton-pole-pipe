04Applications

EVERY GRID.

From wildfire-prone hillsides to hurricane-battered coastlines to offshore platforms — Tekton composite poles are built for the environments where infrastructure failure is not an option.

04.1Where We Operate

THE GRID HAS
HARD PLACES

Wood rots in coastal humidity. Steel corrodes in salt air. Concrete cracks under freeze-thaw cycles. Carbon fiber composite performs across all of them — and under the conditions that break traditional materials, Tekton doesn't just survive. It outlasts.

04.2Application Sectors
Electric Utilities

TRANSMISSION & DISTRIBUTION

The backbone of the American grid runs on poles that are past their design life. Tekton composite poles are a direct form-fit replacement for wood and steel distribution poles — same installation hardware, dramatically longer service life.

  • Distribution lines — 4kV to 34.5kV
  • Transmission support structures
  • Rural electric cooperative networks
  • Urban grid hardening programs
  • Storm restoration — rapid deployment
75+ Year service life — vs. 25–40 for wood
Wildfire-Prone Regions

WHERE WOOD POLES
START FIRES

Wood poles saturated in creosote are an ignition risk in any wildfire corridor. They carry flame from pole to pole. Tekton composite poles are fire-rated — they do not ignite, do not carry flame, and do not add to the fire front. For California, Texas, and the Rocky Mountain West, this is infrastructure risk management.

  • High fire-threat district (HFTD) lines
  • WUI — wildland-urban interface grids
  • Utility ignition risk reduction programs
  • Post-fire rebuild and resilience hardening
FR Fire-rated — does not ignite or propagate flame
Storm & Hurricane Corridors

BUILT FOR CATEGORY 4
AND BEYOND

After every major hurricane, utility crews spend weeks replacing thousands of snapped wood poles. Tekton composite poles are engineered to exceed standard wood deflection ratings — lighter than steel, stronger in lateral load, and immune to the moisture cycles that weaken wood cores over time.

  • Gulf Coast utility hardening
  • Atlantic seaboard distribution upgrades
  • Post-storm rapid rebuild — lighter, faster to set
  • FEMA Hazard Mitigation program eligible
+40 % higher lateral load rating vs. equivalent wood class
Telecommunications

THE LAST MILE
ON COMPOSITE POLES

Telecom networks share utility pole infrastructure across millions of miles of right-of-way. The same pole that carries power often carries fiber, coax, and wireless equipment. Tekton's fully dielectric composite eliminates induced current risk and electromagnetic interference — critical as 5G small cells and fiber-to-the-home deployments expand.

  • Fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) deployments
  • 5G small cell mounting structures
  • Rural broadband expansion corridors
  • Joint use pole attachment programs
100% Dielectric — zero EMI, zero induced current risk
Oil, Gas & Industrial

CHEMICAL ENVIRONMENTS
THAT DESTROY STEEL

Refineries, chemical plants, and offshore platforms operate in corrosion environments that shorten steel pole life dramatically. Tekton composite is chemically inert — immune to the acids, alkalis, and hydrocarbons that attack metals. Where steel corrodes in three years, Tekton remains unchanged at 30.

  • Refinery and chemical plant distribution
  • Offshore platform electrical structures
  • Marine and coastal industrial facilities
  • Mining and heavy industrial sites
0 Corrosion rate — chemically inert composite
Coastal & Wetland Environments

WHERE CHEMICALS
MUST NOT REACH WATER

Wood poles installed in wetlands, floodplains, and coastal zones leach creosote and arsenic directly into ecologically sensitive water systems. Regulatory pressure on chemical pole treatment in these zones is increasing. Tekton is the only pole that has nothing to leach — zero chemical content, zero regulatory exposure.

  • Tidal and estuarine utility corridors
  • Wetland mitigation area crossings
  • Coastal floodplain distribution lines
  • Environmentally sensitive right-of-way
0 Chemical leachate — safe in any water-adjacent installation

THE PIPE SIDE
OF THE EQUATION

Tekton's composite technology doesn't stop at poles. The same material science that builds superior utility poles produces pipe systems for demanding industrial and infrastructure applications.

Water Infrastructure

MUNICIPAL & RURAL WATER

Composite pipe eliminates corrosion in water distribution — no rust, no scale buildup, no chemical leaching into potable water systems.

Industrial Process

CHEMICAL TRANSPORT

High-resistance composite pipe for aggressive chemical transport environments where metal pipe fails within years.

Energy Sector

Infrastructure
