Electric Utilities — T&D Networks

The largest single opportunity for Tekton deployment. Over 180 million wood poles in the United States power transmission and distribution (T&D) networks are aging, with 45% already past their design life. Tekton poles are the drop-in replacement the industry has been waiting for — standards-compliant, dramatically faster to produce, and with a lifespan more than double the wood poles they replace.

Every utility company in America is facing the same math: aging infrastructure, accelerating extreme weather, skyrocketing maintenance costs, and regulatory pressure to harden the grid. Tekton solves all four — simultaneously.

Wildfire Defense — WUI Zones

California alone has seen over 1,500 wildfires linked to power lines since 2015. The Dixie Fire, the Maui wildfires, PG&E's liability catastrophe — the pattern is clear. Wooden utility poles in fire-prone terrain are not just vulnerable. They are accelerants.

Tekton poles do not combust. In a Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) corridor, replacing wooden poles with Tekton infrastructure means the power line becomes a firebreak instead of a fuse. The implications for California, Oregon, Washington, Colorado, Texas, and every other fire-prone state are profound.

Telecom, Broadband & 5G

America's broadband infrastructure mandate — connecting rural communities, small towns, and underserved urban areas — runs on poles. The USDA Rural Utilities Service (RUS) program, the FCC's rural broadband initiatives, and the private sector buildout of 5G networks all depend on fast, affordable, durable pole infrastructure.

Tekton's lightweight construction, rapid production, and long service life make it uniquely suited to broadband expansion projects where speed to deployment, total cost of ownership, and minimal future maintenance are critical success factors.

Municipal & Government Infrastructure

City governments, county utilities, state departments of transportation, and federal infrastructure programs are all facing the same infrastructure replacement cycle — and all are under growing pressure to make responsible, forward-looking materials choices.

Tekton's zero-chemical, zero-deforestation, low-carbon profile makes it a natural fit for ESG-conscious procurement. Its long service life reduces lifecycle cost. Its domestic manufacturing supports American jobs and supply chain security. It checks every box a modern government procurement process demands.

Hurricane & Storm Hardening

Hurricane Ian knocked out power to 2.5 million Florida customers. Hurricane Ida left over 1 million Louisiana residents without power for two weeks or longer. Hurricane Harvey's pole damage in Texas was still being cleared years later. Every major storm event produces the same headlines — and the same bill.

Tekton poles are engineered for excellent extreme weather performance — rated higher than wood and composite alternatives in hurricane-force wind and impact scenarios. They don't rot, they don't absorb moisture, and they don't become structural liabilities after the storm passes.

Pipe Applications — Oil, Gas & Water

The same proprietary Theis Tekton material system that powers the utility pole product line also enables a second major product category: composite piping for oil, gas, and water infrastructure.

Composite pipes offer corrosion resistance, chemical resistance, and service life advantages over steel and concrete piping. The ability to produce pipe on the same manufacturing platform as poles gives Tekton an operational efficiency advantage — and opens a second major addressable market using the same core technology.
